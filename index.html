Our Story

A BLEND OF PASSION & CRAFT

Born from the spirit of excellence that defines Nepal's cricket icon Rohit Paudel, 17 Beans Cafe is more than a café — it's a sanctuary where champions pause, reflect, and fuel their greatness.

Every cup is brewed with the same precision, discipline, and passion that defines Rohit's game. Named after his iconic jersey number, this café embodies the athlete's mindset: perfection in every detail.

The Man Behind The Number

ROHIT
PAUDEL

Nepal's cricket prodigy. A boy who dared to dream on cricket fields, Rohit Paudel became the youngest Test captain in Nepal's history. His journey from the valleys of Nepal to international cricket stages is a testament to discipline, sacrifice, and unyielding passion.

17 Beans Cafe is his vision — a place where that same champion's spirit lives in every espresso shot, every latte art, every moment of calm before the storm.
